Bio

Eli Schalet is a composer based in New York City, writing for chamber ensembles, film and TV, dance, voice, and electronics.

In his final year at Columbia University, he is completing his undergraduate studies in Music and English literature, Italian, and Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian. His work has been performed by The Bergamot Quartet and PinkNoise Ensemble, and has been featured in the award winning short films RABBIT (2022) and When We Move (2026). Eli’s collaborative work composing for dance has been performed at the Boston Conservatory at Berklee, Towson University, and by the New York City-based company Pertazzer Co. He is currently working on his senior thesis, a concept album that returns to the more pop-influenced sound of his early work to study music’s capacity for world building and narration.

During his time at Columbia he has written work in the undergraduate arts publication Ratrock Magazine, now serving on the executive board as co-director of its Writing Team, as well as for CU Records.

Eli’s influences eschew typification, from Romanticism to avant garde to medieval to Yugoslav New Wave and so on. Despite the impossibility of a comprehensive list, some constants include Arca, Chopin, Hildegard von Bingen, Elysia Crampton, Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross, Ravel, Joni Mitchell, Angelo Badalamenti, Ethel Cain, Schumann, Caroline Polachek, Grouper, Lana Del Rey, Squarepusher, and Devonté Hynes.
